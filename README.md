# Walmart-Sales-Challenge

# Sales Performance Analysis and Demand Forecasting at Walmart

#Historical Background
Walmart was founded in 1962 by Sam Walton with a simple but powerful vision: make everyday products affordable for ordinary people. From a single discount store in Rogers, Walmart grew into the world’s 
largest retailer by mastering low-cost operations, supply chain efficiency, and customer-focused pricing. Beyond retail dominance, the company has become a global, data-driven  enterprise that uses
technology and analytics to forecast demand, manage inventory, and enhance customer experience  in an increasingly competitive market.

#Project Overview
This project looks at Walmart’s historical weekly sales data to understand how the business has performed, spot key trends, and improve future sales forecasting.
It focuses on a common retail challenge: making sure products are available when customers need them, without overstocking or wasting resources. When demand is misjudged, it can lead to
empty shelves, excess inventory, lost sales, and unhappy customers.
The analysis explores how factors like holidays, fuel prices, unemployment, and the consumer price index affect sales across different stores and departments. The goal is to generate useful 
insights that support better planning and decision-making, and ultimately help build a more accurate system for predicting future demand.

#Project Focus
The main focus of this project is sales performance analysis and demand forecasting. This involves studying historical sales records, identifying trends and patterns, comparing performance across 
stores and departments, and developing insights that can help Walmart improve its planning and operations.

#Importance of the Project
This project is important because it addresses a problem that is common in many businesses: the inability to accurately predict demand and respond effectively to changes in sales patterns. By improving
forecasting and understanding the factors that drive sales, Walmart can reduce losses, improve product availability, make better use of resources, and increase customer satisfaction.

Project Objectives:
The objective of this project is to help Walmart improve its sales performance and planning by using historical sales data and forecasting techniques to understand demand patterns, 
identify performance gaps, and support more informed business decisions.

The project aims to achieve the following deliverables:
- Data Consolidation and Preparation
- Sales Performance Analysis
- Trend and Pattern Identification
- Demand Forecasting Model Development
- Store and Department Comparison
- External Factor Analysis
- Dashboard and Reporting


##Project Outcomes:

🔍 𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐈𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭𝐬:	
• 💰 $6.74B total revenue across 45 stores	
• 📈 +7% growth (2010→2011) followed by –18% decline (2011→2012)	
• 📅 Peak months: July, April, June | Lowest: January, November	
• 🏪 Top stores: 20, 4, 14, 13, 2 | Underperformers: 33, 44, 5, 36, 29 need urgent attention	
• 🔗 Sales rise with holidays & fuel prices, drop with CPI & unemployment	
• ⚠️ Consistent sales dip in January & November across all stores

Walmart’s revenue is highly seasonal, volatile, and uneven across stores

🚀 𝐑𝐞𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐝𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬:
